PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Prabhu Arunachalam, PhD

The Arunachalam Lab studies how immune experiences accumulated across the lifespan reshape innate immunity and influence future immune responses. Our work combines human systems immunology, single-cell multiomics, and experimental models to understand immune aging, inflammatory memory, and vaccine immunity.
